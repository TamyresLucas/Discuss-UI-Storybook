New `StimulusCard` (Question Types): the image or video attached to a question, as it appears in the question card. An 80px thumbnail (image, custom icon, or a neutral placeholder), the "AI Moderator" label over a caption (what the moderator says about the stimulus), and a trash button. `onCaptionChange` makes the caption editable in place; `status="error"`, or a thumbnail that fails to load, shows the broken-image state. Additive; nothing existing changes.
